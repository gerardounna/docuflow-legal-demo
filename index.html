Questions

How does the free trial work?

Enter your case details above and receive a personalized legal document via email — free, with no credit card. You'll also get 14 days of full access to generate unlimited documents. If you want to continue after that, subscribe at $497/month.

Is the generated document actually usable?

Yes. The AI is trained on real Texas legal practice and generates documents that match the quality and format of what your associates would produce — with Texas-specific provisions (e.g., TX Bus. & Com. Code for non-competes). You review and approve every document before use.

What types of documents does it handle?

NDAs, employment agreements, visa petitions (H-1B, L-1, employment-based), real estate purchase agreements, corporate formation documents, discovery requests, and more. Each is generated with jurisdiction-specific provisions.

Do I need to install anything?

No. DocuFlow runs entirely in your browser. No software to install, no IT project. Setup takes about 2 hours — upload your firm's templates, the AI trains, and you're ready.

What law firms is this designed for?

Solo practitioners and boutique firms with 2-30 attorneys in Texas, focused on: employment law, immigration, corporate/M&A, real estate, and litigation support.
